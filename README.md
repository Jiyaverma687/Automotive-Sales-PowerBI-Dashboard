# Automotive Sales & Product Performance Dashboard

## Overview
An end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing total automotive 
sales performance, product line profitability, and monthly revenue trends 
against business targets.

## Key Insights
- Analyzed $9.6M in total sales revenue across the business
- Tracked 106,000+ orders across 7 distinct product lines
- Monitored profit margin of $3.83M (39.84%) using dynamic KPI cards
- Visualized monthly revenue trends vs. targets to support strategic 
  planning and forecasting

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- KPI Cards & Slicers
- Interactive Drill-through Reports

Power BI · DAX · Sales Analytics · KPI Monitoring · Data Visualization ·
